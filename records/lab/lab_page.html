Mark 4 plan

Marks 1-3 stay frozen and tracked side by side. Mark 4 is judged recency-weighted and gets its own betting-strategy tournament.

  1. Lab page (this page): every number visible, recency-weighted. Done, first version.
  2. Blind-spot map: where FanDuel's line misses, by situation. First pass done (Blind spots tab); more situations to add.
  3. Book lag: after a role change (a backup promoted, a QB change), how many games until FanDuel's line catches up.
  4. Recency-weighted training: the picker and projections learn mostly from the last one to two seasons.
  5. Strategy tournament per model: straights, parlays, 2 or 3 legs, markets, sides, edge cutoffs, boost use; each candidate keeps its own best.
  6. Case files on every pick: the comps and situations behind each number, shown on the card.
