Slate Cards

2026 weeks 1–5, replayed. Each week the picker was retrained on every line before that week, ranked every under it liked across all markets, and graded against what happened. 1 unit = one flat bet. Parlays are $10 two-leg tickets with a 30% profit boost.

Which version of the card

The same five weeks, run five ways. A caps picks at one per player and two per game. B skips players with fewer than 3 games for their current team. C averages six picker versions instead of trusting one.

The weekly card

Boosted parlays this week

Built from the Top 10 only: each leg paired with the next unused leg from a different game.

Read this before betting

  • Five weeks is a small sample: about 40 Top-10 picks per version. One or two results change the order of the versions.
  • The 2026 files only carry receptions, receiving yards, rush yards and pass yards, so rush and pass attempts (the model’s best markets) never appear here.
  • The picker setup itself was chosen with 2026 weeks 1–5 in view, so these weeks are not a fully clean test. Week 6 on is.
  • Heavy-favourite unders (−180 and worse) win often but pay little; units show the money, the record shows the hit rate.
